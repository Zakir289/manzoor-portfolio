About me

Since childhood, I've loved creating—whether it was making toys from mud or turning everyday things into something fun and useful. People often appreciated my knack for organizing and decorating beautifully, and that became my core strength.

Though I ended up in engineering and a call center job by chance, a lucky encounter with a design team changed my path forever.

For the past 10+ years, I've been designing user experiences across mobile, web, and AR/VR, working with brands like Amazon, QNB, Indegene, Holo Pundits, BNY Mellon, and more. I thrive on solving problems by making things simple, meaningful, and delightful.

Outside of work, I'm fueled by travel, music, DIY crafts, cooking experiments, and staying curious about new design trends.
