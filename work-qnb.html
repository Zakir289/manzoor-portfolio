Project Overview

QNB Digital Banking is a multi-platform banking experience designed to help customers manage their everyday financial activities across mobile, tablet and web.

The project focused on reimagining the experience architecture, navigation, interaction patterns, and visual system across core banking modules—ensuring a consistent experience across platforms, from managing accounts and transfers to cards, loans, investments, and rewards within one cohesive digital ecosystem.

My Role

Led UX/UI design across key QNB Digital Banking journeys, translating business and customer requirements into intuitive, scalable experiences across mobile and web.

Target audiance

Global QNB Customers & customers across markets, with a primary focus on Egypt, supporting both English and Arabic digital banking experiences.

The Building Blocks of QNB Banking Experience

Eight essential banking modules form the foundation of the QNB experience, bringing everyday financial services together in one simple, connected, and consistent platform.

Accounts

View balances, track transactions, and manage your accounts.

Cards

Manage your cards, limits, PINs, and transactions.

Payments

Make payments, pay bills, and manage recurring payments.

Transfers

Send money securely and manage your beneficiaries.

Deposits

Manage your deposits, track balances, and explore savings options.

Investments

Track your portfolio and manage your investments.

Loans

Explore loan options, applications, and repayments.

Support

Get assistance, resolve issues, and contact the bank.
